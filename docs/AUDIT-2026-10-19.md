## Functional status

Click and keyboard movement, follow camera, rectangular collision, target selection and simple local auto-melee exist. Click movement does not find a path around obstacles. Boars are static sprites with HP counters, not complete enemy actors. HP/KI show initial values, not incoming damage or KI consumption. Inventory is empty, skills are locked, journal is informational. Chat is explicitly local. There is no multiplayer server, persistent character, loot, completed quests or progression.

`/test` is a separate private sketch workspace with guarded signed storage operations. Its code and generated Supabase integrations are outside the graphics change. No production database, secret configuration, storage policies or data were changed. This review does not establish the live bucket policy or infrastructure security configuration.
